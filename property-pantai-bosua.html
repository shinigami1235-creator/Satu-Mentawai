The cove

The land sits behind the beach of a U-shaped cove with mature coconut palms. A boat launches safely from the cove and a permanent mooring can be set. The reef in front of the beach has clear water and coral for snorkelling and diving.

Getting there

Rokot Airport on Sipora, completed in 2023, is a 40-minute flight from Padang on Susi Air. The land is a 1-hour drive from the airport. The fast ferry from Padang lands at Tuapejat in the north of Sipora.

Buying as a foreigner

Indonesian law limits freehold ownership by foreigners, so settle how you will hold the land before paying a deposit. A Settle consult covers the ownership options, the notary and the company setup.
