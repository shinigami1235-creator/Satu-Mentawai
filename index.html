Getting to Mentawai

Every trip goes through Padang on the Sumatra coast. Book each step here and we line up the times.

  1. Fly to Padang

    Minangkabau International (PDG) has direct flights from Jakarta and Bali, and from Kuala Lumpur and Singapore.

    Book a flight

  2. Sleep in Padang

    The ferry and early flights leave in the morning, so spend the night before in Padang.

    Book a hotel

  3. Cross to the islands

    Mentawai Fast from Muaro port reaches Tuapejat in about 3.5 hours. Flights to Rokot on Sipora take 40 minutes.

    Book the crossing

  4. Transfer to your stay

    A driver or speedboat meets you at the port or the airport and takes you to the door.

    Book a transfer

The islands

About 70 islands sit 150 km off West Sumatra. Most visitors stay on the four main ones.

  • SiberutThe largest island, with the national park and treks to Mentawai villages. Surf in the Playgrounds region off its south coast.
  • SiporaTuapejat, the regency capital, Rokot Airport and the Bintang and Lances breaks in the south.
  • North and South PagaiFerries land at Sikakap between the two islands. Macaronis is off North Pagai.
