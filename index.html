          Getting to Mentawai

          Every trip goes through Padang on the Sumatra coast. Book each step here and we line up the times.

          1. Fly to Padang

            Minangkabau International (PDG) has direct flights from Jakarta and Bali, and from Kuala Lumpur and Singapore.

            Book a flight

          2. Sleep in Padang

            The ferry and the Susi Air flight leave at 07:00, so spend the night before near the port.

            Book a hotel

          3. Cross to the islands

            Mentawai Fast from Muaro port, 3.5 to 5 hours, or Susi Air to Rokot on Sipora in 40 minutes.

            Book the crossing

          4. Transfer to your stay

            A driver or speedboat meets you at the port or the airport and takes you to the door.

          Before you go

          Six things that catch first-time visitors out.

          Read the full guide
          • Visa

            Apply for the e-VOA online before you fly: IDR 500,000 for 30 days.

          • Surf tax

            IDR 2,000,000 per surfer for every 15 days. Most stays collect it for you.

          • Cash

            Tuapejat has the only ATMs, so take out your rupiah in Padang.

          • Malaria

            Malaria and dengue are present. See a travel doctor and pack DEET.

          • Insurance

            Buy cover for surfing and medical evacuation since serious injuries go to Padang.

          • Signal

            Buy a Telkomsel SIM in Padang. Away from Tuapejat signal is patchy.
