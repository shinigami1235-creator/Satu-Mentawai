Before you go

What a first trip to the Mentawai Islands needs, from the visa to the packing list. Open it once and it stays on your phone for the ferry.

Visa

e-VOA fee
IDR 500,000
Stay
30 days
Extension
30 more days

Most visitors need a visa on arrival. Apply for the electronic version, the e-VOA, at evisa.imigrasi.go.id before you fly since it skips the queue at the airport. Each person needs their own application, children included.

  • Your passport needs 6 months of validity from the day you arrive.
  • ASEAN passports, Thai included, enter visa-free for up to 30 days without an extension.
  • Immigration proposed raising the e-VOA to IDR 750,000 and the airport VOA to IDR 1,000,000 in August 2026. Check the fee when you apply.

Surf tax

Per surfer
IDR 2,000,000
Valid for
15 days
Paid to
Mentawai Regency

Every foreign surfer pays the Mentawai surf tax, renewed for each extra 15 days. Most resorts and charters collect it with your booking, so ask before you pay twice. Rangers check at the main surf areas.

Resorts on land also add the 10% regency hotel and restaurant tax, and some villages ask a small daily donation, such as IDR 30,000 a day at Silabu on North Pagai.

Ferry and flights

Fly to Padang (PDG) and sleep near Muaro port, since the ferry and the island flight leave at 07:00.

Board bags

Board bags travel as freight on the Mentawai Fast for about IDR 220,000 each way, up to 15 kg. Buy the ticket at the port the day before.

Flights to Sipora

Susi Air flies Padang to Rokot Airport on Sipora in 40 minutes on a single-engine Cessna, a few times a week, leaving Padang at 07:00. Baggage limits are tight, so check before bringing boards.

Slow ferry

The KMP Gambolo and Ambu-Ambu car ferries run overnight from Bungus port in Padang to Tuapejat, Siberut, Sikakap and Pokai in about 12 hours.

The Monday Tuapejat sailing was suspended in October 2026 for vessel maintenance. Days and times change at short notice, so confirm with Mentawai Fast before booking flights.

Money

  • Take out the rupiah you need in Padang. Tuapejat has the only working ATMs and a BRI bank, and the rest of the islands run on cash.
  • Indonesian ATMs give the cash before the card, so take your card back before you walk away.
  • Resorts take Visa or Mastercard for the bar tab. Ferries, boatmen, warungs and village donations are cash only.
  • Charters often ask for payment by bank transfer before the trip.

Health and safety

  • Malaria and dengue are present in the Mentawai Islands. See a travel doctor about malaria tablets before you go, and use DEET repellent and long sleeves from dusk.
  • Reef cuts infect fast in the tropics. Clean every cut the same day with antiseptic and keep it dry.
  • Wear reef booties and a helmet on shallow waves like Lances Right and Kandui.
  • Serious injuries go to Padang by boat or plane, so buy travel insurance that covers surfing and medical evacuation.
  • Carry a first-aid kit with antiseptic, dressings and any prescription medicine you take.

Phone and internet

Telkomsel has the widest coverage. Buy the SIM or an eSIM in Padang since phone shops on the islands are few. Signal is good in Tuapejat and patchy elsewhere, most resorts have wifi, and the Siberut forest has no signal and no power, so carry a charged power bank.

Respect the islands

  • Ask before you photograph people, and in an uma ask your guide first.
  • Visit a Mentawai family through a guide who works with them, and bring the gifts the guide suggests.
  • Bring a refillable bottle. Some islands have banned single-use plastic bottles.
  • Pay the village donation where it is asked. It goes to the village whose reef you surf.
  • Dress modestly in Tuapejat and in villages, away from the beach.
  • Don't buy or move coral, shells, turtle products or wild animals.
